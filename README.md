A make.fun entry: `main.ts` (Node 22) writes one one-star houseplant review to `/output/entry.txt`.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), UTF-8, no trailing newline, mode 0644. A guard in the program fails loudly if the line ever leaves the gate's 1–279 character window.

## Entry text

> One star. I was bought to 'bring life to the room', then parked behind the TV, where the only light I get is the glow of my owner's 2 a.m. doomscrolling, and the plastic plant on the shelf gets dusted more often than I get watered. Would not recommend this human to other plants.

279 characters, one line. Built for the judge brief: a clear grievance and a reason for the single star, told entirely through the plant's own view of light, water and being moved — with the fake plant getting better care as the second beat and a real review-format closer.
